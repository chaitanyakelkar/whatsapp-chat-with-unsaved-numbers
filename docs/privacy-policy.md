# Privacy Policy for Chat on Whatsapp

**Effective date:** November 9, 2026

This policy explains how the Android app **Chat on Whatsapp** (package `com.cakelkar.chatonwhatsapp`), made by Chaitanya Kelkar, handles your information. The app lets you open a WhatsApp chat with a phone number without saving it as a contact.

## Summary

- The app does not collect, store on a server, sell or share your personal data.
- The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything over the internet itself.
- It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
- Everything the app does with a phone number happens on your device.

## Information the app uses

**Phone numbers you enter, paste or share.** The app reads the number to work out its country and check its length. This happens only on your device.

**Your clipboard.** When you open the app or return to it, it checks the current clipboard entry for a phone number, so it can fill the number in for you. It reads only the current entry, never your clipboard history, and only uses the text if it contains a valid phone number. Android 12 and newer show a notice whenever this happens.

**Text you share or select.** If you share text with the app, or select text and choose the app from the menu, it looks for a phone number in that text on your device.

## Information stored on your device

The app saves a few settings on your device, so it can remember them between uses:

- the country you last used
- whether **Open automatically** is turned on
- the last copied text that was opened automatically, so the same number isn't opened again every time you start the app

These settings stay on your device. If your phone's backup is turned on, Android may include them in your device backup with your other app data, under your backup provider's terms. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes them.

## When you open a chat

When you tap **Open in WhatsApp**, or a number opens automatically, the app passes the number to WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business or your web browser as a `wa.me` link. From that point, that app's own privacy policy applies, such as [WhatsApp's Privacy Policy](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy).

## Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services. It uses Google's open-source libphonenumber library to understand phone numbers, which runs entirely on your device and sends no data anywhere.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Your choices

- Turn off **Open automatically** in the app at any time.
- Clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app, to remove everything it stored.
